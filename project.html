Chef’s Specials

Starters

  • Paneer Tikka
    - Marinated cottage cheese cubes grilled in a tandoor
  • Dahi Kebab
    - Hung curd-based patties with spices, soft and creamy inside
  • Onion Pakora
    - Crispy gram flour-coated onion fritters
  • Stuffed Mushrooms
    - Mushrooms filled with spiced veggies and cheese, grilled or baked

Drinks

  • Masala Chai
    -Spiced Indian tea made with milk, and a mix of cardamom, ginger, and cloves
  • Sweet Lassi
    -A chilled yogurt-based drink, sweetened and sometimes flavored with rosewater
  • Old Monk
    –Rich and smooth dark rum with caramel notes
  • Smirnoff Vodka
    –Versatile vodka with a clean, crisp taste

Indian Dishes

  • Paneer Butter Masala
    –Creamy tomato gravy with soft cubes of paneer
  • Chicken Tikka Masala
    –Tender chicken in a rich, spiced curry sauce.
  • Butter Chicken
    –Mild, buttery, and irresistibly smooth chicken curry.
  • Kadai Paneer
    –Paneer cubes tossed with bell peppers in a spiced tomato gravy.

International Dishes

  • Spaghetti Bolognese
    –Classic Italian spaghetti in a hearty meat sauce
  • Grilled Chicken with Herbs
    –Juicy grilled chicken seasoned with aromatic herbs
  • Chicken Stroganoff
    –Creamy mushroom sauce served with chicken and rice or pasta
  • Garlic Bread with Cheese
    –Toasted baguette slices loaded with melted cheese

Desserts

  • Gulab Jamun
    –Deep-fried milk dumplings soaked in warm sugar syrup
  • Rasgulla
    –Spongy cottage cheese balls in a light sugary syrup
  • Apple Pie
    –Baked pastry filled with spiced apples and a golden crust
  • Ice Cream Sundae
    –Scoops of ice cream topped with sauces, nuts, and a cherry

Sides

  • Green Salad
    –A dish made up primarily of lettuce, spinach, and arugula and a dressing.
  • Garlic Bread
    –Bread that has been brushed with garlic butter or oil and then baked or grilled
  • Onion Rings
    –Sliced onions that are battered and deep-fried until crispy
  • Caesar Salad
    –A green salad of romaine lettuce and croutons dressed with Parmesan cheese, lemon juice, olive oil, egg, Worcestershire sauce, and black pepper
