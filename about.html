Palm and Pine

Our Story

Palm and Pine began as a cherished family dream, and since opening its doors in 2012, it has grown into a beloved gathering place for generations. With a focus on warmth, care, and unforgettable flavors, we've welcomed guests like family and built lasting memories through every meal we serve.

Our Mission

We’re committed to crafting meals using fresh, locally-sourced ingredients, celebrating the beauty of simplicity and the soul of every dish. Our goal is to offer more than just a meal—we strive to create a memorable experience for every customer who walks through our doors.
